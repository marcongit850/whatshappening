What's Happening

Marketing, media, and brand development

What's Happening is a marketing and media company focused on building brands, developing digital properties, creating advertising opportunities, and helping businesses connect with the right audiences.

We develop our own websites, directories, guides, and media brands while also creating custom marketing and advertising programs for businesses.

What We Do

  • Marketing

    Strategy, campaigns, digital marketing, brand development, and creative concepts designed to help businesses grow, improve visibility, and reach more customers.

  • Digital Media

    We create and operate targeted websites, directories, guides, and online platforms built around specific audiences, industries, and communities.

  • Advertising

    Digital advertising, sponsorships, featured placements, partnerships, and custom advertising opportunities designed to connect businesses with relevant audiences.

  • Creative & Brand Development

    Branding, websites, content, graphics, campaign concepts, and creative development that help turn ideas into recognizable, marketable brands.

Our Background

Built on decades of experience

What's Happening has roots in publishing, advertising, promotional products, and local media.

Over the years, parts of the company were sold as those businesses grew and evolved. Today, What's Happening is focused on the next generation of the company: digital media, marketing, advertising, brand development, and new business concepts.

That history gives us a practical understanding of how to build products, reach audiences, create advertising opportunities, and turn ideas into businesses.

A Look Back at What We Built

Over the years, we’ve created magazines, apartment guides, maps, coupon publications, mobile advertising programs, and branded promotional products for businesses and organizations across a wide range of markets.
